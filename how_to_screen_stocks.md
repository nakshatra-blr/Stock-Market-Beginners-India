# How to Screen Stocks on Screener.in

## Go to screener.in

## Filters for Quality Stocks:
```n- Market Cap > 5000 Cr (large/mid cap)
- ROE > 15%
- Debt/Equity < 1
- Profit Growth 3yrs > 10%
- Revenue Growth 3yrs > 10%
- Promoter Holding > 50%
```n
## Save this screen and check weekly for new entries
