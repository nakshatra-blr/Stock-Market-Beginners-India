# Stock Investment Checklist

Before buying any stock, check:

## Fundamentals
- [ ] Revenue growing > 10% for 3 years
- [ ] Profit margins stable or improving
- [ ] Debt/Equity < 1
- [ ] ROE > 15%
- [ ] Promoter holding > 50%
- [ ] No pledged shares

## Technicals
- [ ] Stock above 200-day MA
- [ ] RSI between 30-70
- [ ] Volume increasing on up days

## Risk Management
- [ ] Position size < 5% of portfolio
- [ ] Stop loss set at 10-15% below entry
- [ ] Target return at least 2x the risk
