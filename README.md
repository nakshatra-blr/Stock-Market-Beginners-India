## 🏦 What is the Stock Market?

A marketplace where shares (ownership pieces) of publicly listed companies are bought and sold.

| Exchange | Full Name | Key Index | Companies |
|----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **BSE** | Bombay Stock Exchange | Sensex (30 stocks) | 5000+ listed |
| **NSE** | National Stock Exchange | Nifty 50 (50 stocks) | 2000+ listed |

### Market Timings
```
Pre-open:     9:00 AM - 9:15 AM
Trading:      9:15 AM - 3:30 PM
Post-close:   3:30 PM - 4:00 PM
Days:         Monday to Friday (except holidays)
```

## 📖 Key Terms You MUST Know

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Share/Stock** | A small piece of ownership in a company |
| **Sensex** | Index of top 30 companies on BSE |
| **Nifty 50** | Index of top 50 companies on NSE |
| **Bull Market** | Market going UP (optimism) |
| **Bear Market** | Market going DOWN (pessimism) |
| **Portfolio** | Collection of all your investments |
| **Dividend** | Profit shared by company to shareholders |
| **Market Cap** | Total value of company = Share price x Total shares |
| **P/E Ratio** | Price/Earnings — how expensive is the stock |
| **EPS** | Earnings Per Share — profit per share |
| **IPO** | Initial Public Offering — company lists for first time |
| **Demat Account** | Digital account to hold shares |
| **Broker** | Platform to buy/sell (Zerodha, Groww, Angel One) |
| **SEBI** | Securities & Exchange Board of India (regulator) |
| **Circuit Limit** | Max price movement allowed in a day (5%, 10%, 20%) |

## 🚀 How to Start (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Open a Demat + Trading Account

| Broker | Charges | Best For |
|--------|---------|----------|
| **Zerodha** | Rs.0 delivery, Rs.20/trade | Most popular, reliable |
| **Groww** | Rs.0 delivery, Rs.20/trade | Beginner-friendly app |
| **Angel One** | Rs.0 delivery, Rs.20/trade | Good research tools |
| **Upstox** | Rs.0 delivery, Rs.20/trade | Budget-friendly |

**Documents needed:** PAN Card, Aadhaar, Bank account, Selfie, Signature

### Step 2: Add Funds
- Transfer money from bank to trading account (UPI/Net Banking)
- Start with Rs.5,000-10,000 (money you can afford to lose while learning)

### Step 3: Learn Before You Invest
- Paper trade for 1-2 months (practice without real money)
- Study at least 5 companies before buying anything
- Start with large-cap stocks or Index Funds

### Step 4: Buy Your First Stock
```
Open app → Search company → Check price → Buy → Done!
Your shares appear in Demat account next day (T+1 settlement)
```

## 📊 Fundamental Analysis

*Analyzing a company's actual business to determine if the stock is worth buying*

### Key Metrics to Check

| Metric | What it Tells You | Good Sign |
|--------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Revenue Growth** | Is business growing? | >10% YoY |
| **Net Profit Margin** | How profitable? | >10% (industry dependent) |
| **P/E Ratio** | Is it expensive? | Compare with industry avg |
| **ROE** | Returns to shareholders | >15% |
| **Debt/Equity** | How much debt? | <1 (lower is better) |
| **Promoter Holding** | Owner's confidence | >50% (ideally increasing) |
| **Free Cash Flow** | Actual cash generated | Positive and growing |

### Checklist Before Buying Any Stock
```
☐ Revenue growing consistently for 3-5 years?
☐ Profit growing (not just revenue)?
☐ Low or decreasing debt?
☐ Promoter holding above 50%?
☐ ROE above 15%?
☐ Do I understand what this company does?
☐ Am I willing to hold for 3+ years?
☐ Is the P/E reasonable vs peers?
```

### Where to Check Company Data
- **Screener.in** — Best free tool for Indian stocks
- **Moneycontrol** — News + financials
- **Tickertape** — Visual analysis
- **Annual Reports** — Direct from company website

## 📉 Technical Analysis Basics

*Reading price charts to predict short-term movement*

### Key Concepts

| Concept | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **Support** | Price level where stock stops falling (buyers step in) |
| **Resistance** | Price level where stock stops rising (sellers step in) |
| **Volume** | Number of shares traded — high volume = strong move |
| **Moving Average** | Average price over X days (50-day, 200-day) |
| **RSI** | Relative Strength Index — Overbought (>70) or Oversold (<30) |
| **Candlestick** | Shows Open, High, Low, Close for each day |

### Beginner Rule
```
If stock price > 200-day Moving Average → Uptrend (consider buying)
If stock price < 200-day Moving Average → Downtrend (avoid/wait)
```

## 🛒 Types of Orders

| Order Type | What it Does | When to Use |
|-----------|-------------|-------------|
| **Market Order** | Buy/sell at current price immediately | When speed matters |
| **Limit Order** | Buy/sell only at YOUR specified price | When price matters |
| **Stop Loss** | Automatically sell if price drops to X | Protect from big losses |
| **GTT** | Good Till Triggered — auto-executes later | Set and forget |

### Golden Rule: ALWAYS Set a Stop Loss
```
Buy price: Rs.100
Stop Loss: Rs.90 (10% below)
Target: Rs.130 (30% above)

Risk:Reward = 1:3 (ideal minimum is 1:2)
```

## ⚖️ Index Funds vs Direct Stocks

| | Index Fund | Direct Stocks |
|---|---|---|
| **Risk** | Medium (diversified) | High (single company) |
| **Knowledge needed** | Low | High |
| **Time needed** | 0 (passive) | Daily monitoring |
| **Returns** | 12-14% avg | Unlimited (or loss) |
| **Best for** | Beginners, busy people | Experienced investors |

**Start with Index Funds → Learn → Then try Direct Stocks**

## 🧾 Taxation on Stocks (India)

| Type | Holding Period | Tax Rate |
|------|---------------|----------|
| **STCG** (Short-term) | < 1 year | 20% |
| **LTCG** (Long-term) | > 1 year | 12.5% (above Rs.1.25 lakh) |
| **Intraday Profits** | Same day | Taxed as business income (slab rate) |
| **Dividends** | — | Taxed at your income slab rate |

**Tax-free:** LTCG up to Rs.1.25 lakh/year is EXEMPT (FY 2025-26)

## ❌ Common Mistakes

```
1. ❌ Investing without learning (gambling, not investing)
2. ❌ Following WhatsApp/Telegram tips (99% are scams)
3. ❌ No stop loss (one bad stock can wipe out gains)
4. ❌ Averaging down without analysis (catching falling knives)
5. ❌ Panic selling during market dips (dips are opportunities)
6. ❌ Checking portfolio every hour (leads to emotional decisions)
7. ❌ Putting all money in one stock (no diversification)
8. ❌ Trading with borrowed money (recipe for disaster)
9. ❌ Expecting quick returns (market is for patient people)
10. ❌ Not tracking your trades (no learning from mistakes)
```
